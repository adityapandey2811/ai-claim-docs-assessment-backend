SURYODAYA HEALTH SHIELD POLICY — GENERAL EXCLUSIONS

1.1 Pre-Existing Diseases: Any condition, ailment, injury, or disease that was
diagnosed, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within 48
months prior to the policy's first inception date, is excluded from coverage
for the first 24 months of continuous policy coverage. After 24 months of
continuous renewal without a break, pre-existing conditions become covered as
per standard waiting period rules.

1.2 Cosmetic and Aesthetic Treatment: Treatment for cosmetic, aesthetic, or
plastic surgery is excluded unless required to treat injuries sustained in an
accident covered under this policy, or to correct a congenital anomaly in a
covered dependent child.

1.3 Self-Inflicted Injury: Injuries that are intentionally self-inflicted,
including attempted suicide, are not covered under this policy, whether the
insured is medically sound or of unsound mind at the time.

1.4 Substance-Related Treatment: Treatment for conditions arising directly from
the use of alcohol, illegal drugs, or substances not prescribed by a registered
medical practitioner is excluded.

1.5 Dental and Vision Care: Routine dental treatment and vision correction
(spectacles, contact lenses, refractive surgery) are excluded unless necessitated
by an accidental injury covered under this policy.

1.6 Maternity Waiting Period: Maternity-related expenses, including normal
delivery, caesarean section, and pre/post-natal expenses, are covered only
after a continuous waiting period of 36 months from the policy's first
inception date.

1.7 War and Nuclear Risks: Injuries or illnesses arising from war, invasion,
act of foreign enemy, nuclear radiation, or biological/chemical warfare are
excluded.

1.8 Experimental Treatment: Any treatment, drug, or procedure that is
experimental, investigational, or not approved by recognized medical
authorities in India is excluded from coverage.
