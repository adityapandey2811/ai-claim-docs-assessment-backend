SECTION 1 — GENERAL EXCLUSIONS
================================================================

1.1 Pre-Existing Diseases: Any condition, ailment, injury, or disease that was
diagnosed, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within 48
months prior to the policy's first inception date, is excluded from coverage
for the first 24 months of continuous policy coverage. After 24 months of
continuous renewal without a break, pre-existing conditions become covered as
per standard waiting period rules.

1.2 Cosmetic and Aesthetic Treatment: Treatment for cosmetic, aesthetic, or
plastic surgery is excluded unless required to treat injuries sustained in an
accident covered under this policy, or to correct a congenital anomaly in a
covered dependent child.

1.3 Self-Inflicted Injury: Injuries that are intentionally self-inflicted,
including attempted suicide, are not covered under this policy, whether the
insured is medically sound or of unsound mind at the time.

1.4 Substance-Related Treatment: Treatment for conditions arising directly from
the use of alcohol, illegal drugs, or substances not prescribed by a registered
medical practitioner is excluded.

1.5 Dental and Vision Care: Routine dental treatment and vision correction
(spectacles, contact lenses, refractive surgery) are excluded unless necessitated
by an accidental injury covered under this policy.

1.6 Maternity Waiting Period: Maternity-related expenses, including normal
delivery, caesarean section, and pre/post-natal expenses, are covered only
after a continuous waiting period of 36 months from the policy's first
inception date.

1.7 War and Nuclear Risks: Injuries or illnesses arising from war, invasion,
act of foreign enemy, nuclear radiation, or biological/chemical warfare are
excluded.

1.8 Experimental Treatment: Any treatment, drug, or procedure that is
experimental, investigational, or not approved by recognized medical
authorities in India is excluded from coverage.

SECTION 2 — ROOM RENT AND ASSOCIATED SUB-LIMITS
================================================================

2.1 Room Rent Cap: Room, boarding, and nursing expenses are payable up to a
maximum of the daily room rent limit specified in the policy schedule for the
insured's chosen plan tier:
    - Silver Plan: Rs. 4,000 per day
    - Gold Plan: Rs. 7,000 per day
    - Platinum Plan: No sub-limit (room rent payable as actually incurred,
      up to a single private room)

2.2 Proportionate Deduction Clause: If the insured is admitted to a room whose
rent exceeds the applicable daily limit under their plan tier, ALL associated
hospitalization expenses (excluding pharmacy and diagnostics, which are
reimbursed in full subject to overall sum insured) — including doctor's fees,
nursing charges, and other room-linked charges — shall be reduced in the same
proportion as the actual room rent charged bears to the entitled room rent
limit. This is a standard proportionate deduction clause common to Indian
health insurance products.

Example: If a Silver Plan holder (Rs. 4,000/day limit) is admitted to a room
costing Rs. 8,000/day, the proportion is 4,000/8,000 = 50%. All eligible
room-linked charges are reimbursed at 50% of the actual billed amount.

2.3 ICU Charges: Intensive Care Unit charges are payable in full without
application of the room rent proportionate deduction clause, subject to the
overall sum insured limit.

SECTION 3 — CLAIM INTIMATION AND DOCUMENTATION TIMELINES
================================================================

3.1 Planned (Elective) Hospitalization: The insured or their representative
must intimate the insurer at least 48 hours before the scheduled date of
admission.

3.2 Emergency Hospitalization: In case of emergency admission, intimation to
the insurer must be made within 24 hours of admission.

3.3 Final Claim Document Submission: All final claim documents, including
original bills, discharge summary, investigation reports, and prescriptions,
must be submitted to the insurer within 15 days from the date of discharge
from the hospital. Claims submitted beyond 30 days from discharge without a
valid, documented reason (such as prolonged unconsciousness of the insured)
will not be entertained.

3.4 Required Documents for Claim Processing:
    - Duly filled and signed claim form
    - Original hospital bill with itemized break-up
    - Discharge summary from the treating hospital
    - Original prescriptions and pharmacy bills
    - Investigation/diagnostic reports
    - KYC documents of the insured
    - Cancelled cheque or bank details for reimbursement claims

SECTION 4 — SUM INSURED AND CO-PAYMENT
================================================================

4.1 Sum Insured: The maximum liability of the insurer for any single policy
year is the sum insured amount specified in the policy schedule, per insured
member (individual basis) or shared across the family (floater basis), as
applicable.

4.2 Co-Payment: A mandatory co-payment of 10% applies to all claims for
insured persons aged 61 years and above at the time of hospitalization. This
means the insured bears 10% of the eligible claim amount, and the insurer
pays the remaining 90%, subject to the sum insured limit.

4.3 Zone-Based Co-Payment: For treatment availed in a Zone A city (as defined
in the policy schedule) by an insured whose policy was purchased under a
Zone B or Zone C premium rate, an additional co-payment of 20% applies in
addition to any age-based co-payment.

================================================================
SECTION 5 — POLICY VALIDITY AND RENEWAL
================================================================

5.1 Coverage Period: This policy provides coverage strictly between the
Policy Start Date and Policy End Date as specified in the policy schedule.
Any hospitalization event where the date of admission falls outside this
period is not covered under this policy term, regardless of when the claim
is filed.

5.2 Grace Period: A grace period of 30 days from the policy expiry date is
permitted for renewal. However, if a claim event (hospitalization) occurs
during this grace period before the policy is actually renewed, it is NOT
covered — the grace period only preserves continuity benefits for a
subsequent renewal, it does not extend active coverage.

5.3 Free Look Period: The insured may cancel the policy within 15 days of
receiving the policy document if they disagree with any terms, subject to
deduction of proportionate risk premium and any expenses incurred by the
insurer.

================================================================
SECTION 6 — CLAIM SETTLEMENT DECISION FRAMEWORK
================================================================

6.1 A claim will typically proceed toward approval when: all submitted
documents are complete and consistent, the treatment dates fall within the
active policy period, the claimed amount reconciles with itemized hospital
charges, and no excluded condition or waiting-period restriction applies.

6.2 A claim will typically require rejection or further scrutiny when: the
treatment date precedes the policy start date or follows the policy end date,
the claimed amount is inconsistent with the itemized bill without
satisfactory explanation, policy or claimant identification numbers do not
match across submitted documents, or the claim falls within an exclusion or
unexpired waiting period listed in Section 1.

6.3 All claim decisions communicated by any automated or AI-assisted system
are recommendations only. Final adjudication authority rests solely with an
authorized human claims officer of Suryodaya General Insurance Company
Limited.
