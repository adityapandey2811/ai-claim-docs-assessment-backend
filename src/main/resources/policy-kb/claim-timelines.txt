SURYODAYA HEALTH SHIELD POLICY — CLAIM TIMELINES, DOCUMENTATION, AND POLICY VALIDITY

3.1 Planned (Elective) Hospitalization: The insured or their representative
must intimate the insurer at least 48 hours before the scheduled date of
admission.

3.2 Emergency Hospitalization: In case of emergency admission, intimation to
the insurer must be made within 24 hours of admission.

3.3 Final Claim Document Submission: All final claim documents, including
original bills, discharge summary, investigation reports, and prescriptions,
must be submitted to the insurer within 15 days from the date of discharge
from the hospital. Claims submitted beyond 30 days from discharge without a
valid, documented reason (such as prolonged unconsciousness of the insured)
will not be entertained.

3.4 Required Documents for Claim Processing:
    - Duly filled and signed claim form
    - Original hospital bill with itemized break-up
    - Discharge summary from the treating hospital
    - Original prescriptions and pharmacy bills
    - Investigation/diagnostic reports
    - KYC documents of the insured
    - Cancelled cheque or bank details for reimbursement claims

5.1 Coverage Period: This policy provides coverage strictly between the
Policy Start Date and Policy End Date as specified in the policy schedule.
Any hospitalization event where the date of admission falls outside this
period is not covered under this policy term, regardless of when the claim
is filed.

5.2 Grace Period: A grace period of 30 days from the policy expiry date is
permitted for renewal. However, if a claim event (hospitalization) occurs
during this grace period before the policy is actually renewed, it is NOT
covered — the grace period only preserves continuity benefits for a
subsequent renewal, it does not extend active coverage.

6.1 A claim will typically proceed toward approval when: all submitted
documents are complete and consistent, the treatment dates fall within the
active policy period, the claimed amount reconciles with itemized hospital
charges, and no excluded condition or waiting-period restriction applies.

6.2 A claim will typically require rejection or further scrutiny when: the
treatment date precedes the policy start date or follows the policy end date,
the claimed amount is inconsistent with the itemized bill without
satisfactory explanation, policy or claimant identification numbers do not
match across submitted documents, or the claim falls within an exclusion or
unexpired waiting period listed in the exclusions section.

6.3 All claim decisions communicated by any automated or AI-assisted system
are recommendations only. Final adjudication authority rests solely with an
authorized human claims officer of Suryodaya General Insurance Company
Limited.
